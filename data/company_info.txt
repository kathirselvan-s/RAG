We specialize in building intelligent systems, RAG applications, computer vision, and enterprise software.

1. NovaRAG - Our flagship Retrieval-Augmented Generation platform for enterprise knowledge bases.
2. VisionAI - Computer vision solutions for manufacturing quality control.
3. AutoML Suite - Automated machine learning platform for non-experts.

Annual Revenue (2025): $45 Million
Clients: 120+ companies across India, Singapore, and Middle East.
