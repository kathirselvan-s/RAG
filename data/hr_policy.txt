HR Policy Handbook - TechNova Solutions

Leave Policy:
- Casual Leave: 12 days per year
- Sick Leave: 10 days per year
- Privilege Leave: 15 days per year (can be carried forward up to 30 days)
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks as per Indian law
- Paternity Leave: 15 days

Work From Home:
- Hybrid model: Minimum 3 days in office per week
- Full WFH available for special cases with manager approval

Working Hours:
- Core hours: 10 AM to 5 PM IST
- Flexible start between 8 AM - 11 AM

Performance Review:
- Bi-annual reviews in April and October
- 360-degree feedback system

Code of Conduct:
- Zero tolerance for harassment
- Strict data privacy and confidentiality rules
- All employees must complete annual security training
